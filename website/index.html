BookPlayer (legacy)
 
Suspended by Google in February 2026 because its worldwide radio directory included a few stations that Google Play policies do not allow. These stations are now blocked in the new BookPlayer. Sadly, all of its Play Store history went with it: ratings, reviews, download count… The new BookPlayer started again from zero in 2026. :-(
 
Legacy users: if you installed it from an APK, you can still update it from this page.
If you installed it from the Google Play Store, it can no longer be updated. I recommend switching to the new BookPlayer or BookPlayer Pure.
